What happens on a call

Booked, not noted

If you book

It finds out what's wrong, confirms the number you're calling from, takes the address, offers two arrival windows from the real calendar, reads the whole thing back once, and books it. You get a text confirmation to the number you called from. The appointment lands on the company's calendar while you're still on the line.

If it's an emergency

No heat in a freeze, no cooling with someone vulnerable at home, gas, a carbon-monoxide alarm, water pouring out of the furnace: it says what to do first — leave, call 911, find the shutoff — then offers to connect you to the on-call tech right now.

If you ask for a human

It says "one moment" and transfers. It never argues, never asks why, never offers a callback instead. On the demo line that rings a real phone, so be nice.

Afterwards

The call is transcribed and summarised in the company's call log: what you said, what it did, the outcome. That log is what the owner reads with their coffee, and it's what a Bregstar agency shows its clients every Monday.
